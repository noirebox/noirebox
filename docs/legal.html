03 The tool records locally — by design

NoireBox, the product documented here, is a flight data recorder for AI agents: it writes its hash-chained journal on the operator's own machine. Nothing is uploaded to us — there is no server to upload to. Anchoring to external timestamp authorities is opt-in and performed by the operator, not by the project.
